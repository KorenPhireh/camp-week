ARENA

Blast your friends. Grab power-ups. Steal the crown. 👑

How to play
  • Move: WASD / arrows. Aim: mouse. Shoot: hold left click. Dash: Space or right click.
  • Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and shoots, DASH button dashes.
  • Grab power-ups: 🔱 triple shot, ⚡ rapid fire, 🚀 rockets, 🎱 bouncy bullets, 🛡️ shield, ❤️ health, 👟 speed.
  • Each blast = 1 point. The leader wears the 👑, and taking them down is worth 2. First to 20 (or most points after 3:00) wins.
  • Last 30 seconds is FRENZY: everyone shoots twice as fast.
  • Enter to chat, Tab for the scoreboard, M to mute.
